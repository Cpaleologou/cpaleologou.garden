# DAILY MAINTENANCE

1. Write in Obsidian as usual.

2. Update your site:

   Open Terminal in your Quartz folder.

   Run: npm run deploy

   (This wipes the old snapshot and pulls a fresh copy from iCloud).

3. Preview your site:

   Run: npx quartz build --serve

   Go to http://localhost:8080 in your browser.
