Marxist philosophy attempts to understand and forecast human history through distinct phases of civilization, centred around production and resource allocation.

The 6 key phased are:

1. Primitive Communism
2. Slave Society
3. Feudalism
4. Capitalism
5. Socialism
6. Communism
