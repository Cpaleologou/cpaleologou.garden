Even if the sycophancy of chatbots is resolved, it will not curb the attraction that intellectuals have towards the technology.
Intellectualism is a lonely hobby.

First, it is difficult to find anyone who is patient or interested enough to here all you have to say on a subject, or is interested enough to here you think through partially formed ideas, rather than the complete thing.

Second, communicating ideas to others, especially those with different backgrounds or languages, is difficult. 

Chatbots are extremely good at deciphering what you mean and giving a response and have infinite patience.

It is really the ultimate bait for people who enjoy the act of pondering.
