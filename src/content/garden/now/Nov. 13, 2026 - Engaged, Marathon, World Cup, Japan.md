## Relationships

I'm engaged! Well, we're engaged! I proposed to Linda on our trip to Japan (see below). Obviously, I knew I was going to propose on the trip, but I had no clue when or where. Not really much of a planner. Anyway, I had just three rules for when/where I would propose:

1. Not raining. I don't mind rain, but Linda hates it.
2. Not crowded. Even though I know Linda would say yes, I'm not one to call unneeded attention to myself in public.
3. Nice place. Obviously.

What I didn't expect was how hard it was to find all three of these together. Our first week in Tokyo had wonderful weather. But good luck finding somewhere nice and not crowded. Our second week rained quite a bit, and I struggled to find a place that felt right. Fortunately, we stubbled upon a private garden on a day-trip to the outskirts of Kyoto and I made the executive decision then and there. Photos courtesy of Ari (Linda's twin sister).
