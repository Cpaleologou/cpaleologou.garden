## Summary
**The Most Important Thing** is a collection of essays and reflections on lessons learned about investing and markets through Marks' career as a value investor.
Marks is strong believer in the reality that markets can be inefficient, that contrarian thinking is the only way to beat the market, and that understanding where we are in the credit cycle is imperative for long-term survival.
Beating the market over the long term is not easy, but by staying unemotional, thinking one step ahead and being patient, it is possible. The book shares both lessons and frameworks for how to do just that.
