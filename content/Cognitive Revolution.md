# Cognitive Revolution
Created: 2024-03-25 06:31

In Sapiens, the first 1-2 chapters discuss the Cognitive Revolution. The period of time in which the brain of Homo Sapiens and our ancestors grew rapidly and allowed us to develop a number of key abilities, including:
- advanced tool use, 
- The ability to imagine abstract theories and concepts
- The ability to communicate extremely effectively with each other.
