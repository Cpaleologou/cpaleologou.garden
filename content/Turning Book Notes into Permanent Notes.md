# Turning Book Notes into Permanent Notes
Created: 09:46 09:46

Once you've finished the book, export your notes and have a read through them. The goal here is to try to identify which notes are most impactful or relevant to you. 
A good rule of thumb would be if the note written in your own words stands out to you. If that's the case, the idea, both in the authors words and in your own interpretation of it, has been relevant to you in some way twice. 
Those notes that really stand out should be converted to permanent notes. 
As you turn the note into a permanent, rewrite it again, but this time with more detail and with connections.
