# The Absurd

The absurd, as defined by Albert Camus, is the empty chasm between our soul's desire for meaning, and the lack of a discernible answer from the universe. 
In other words, the absurd is a comparison between what we deeply crave (meaning), and the fact that we cannot find one assuredly.
