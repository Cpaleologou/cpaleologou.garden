# Limited Liability
Created: 2024-03-05 06:43

Limited liability exists to encourage entrepreneurship. It’s a sign of the recognition of the value people provide to an economy. 
By reducing the fear people may have of getting sued and losing everything, the legal concept increases the chances that skilled people will try to start a business.
