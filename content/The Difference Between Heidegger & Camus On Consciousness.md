# The Difference Between Heidegger & Camus On Consciousness

Heidegger, in Being & Time, compares consciousness to a clearing with Being (the universe) steps into to reveal itself.

Camus, in the Myth of Sisyphus, discuss consciousness as a spot light we aim towards phenomena.

The difference is that in Heidegger's models, Being (or the universe) is the active participant, revealing itself to humans.
Whereas in Camus' model, the person is the active participant, aiming itself at the universe to illuminate it.
