# Mobility & Metropolitan Life Drives Social Atomism

Industrialized, capitalist culture has relied on mobility of humans to travel for better work since its beginning. It is not so entrenched in our culture and way of life that many of us have little choice but to move around as we seek a comfortable life.
As we move from our birthplaces, we settle in massive urban environments where the nature of life shifts from intense regular face-to-face interaction, to rare, unfriendly touch points out of chance, requirement or convenience.
