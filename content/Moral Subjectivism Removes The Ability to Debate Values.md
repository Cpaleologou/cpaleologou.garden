# Moral Subjectivism Removes The Ability to Debate Values

The issue with Moral Subjectivism is that values or beliefs can not be debated because a given belief system has not been adopted through logic or experience. 
You can point out logic gaps, or unintended consequences of someones belief, but you cannot convince someone to change their mind, even with sounds logic, because that is not the criteria they are evaluating their options with. 
The only criteria is emotional appeal.
