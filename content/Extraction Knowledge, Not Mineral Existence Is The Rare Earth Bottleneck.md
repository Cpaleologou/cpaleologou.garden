# Extraction Knowledge, Not Mineral Existence Is The Rare Earth Bottleneck

Just having mines that hold or extract rare earth ore is not enough to create a valid operation.
The extremely complex processes of extraction are tightly kept secrets, that even when known, vary from region to region.
So even if minerals are found in North America, for example, we may not have the knowledge to extract them.
