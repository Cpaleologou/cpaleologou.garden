# Categorizing Notes in Obsidian
Created: 09:41 09:41

The idea is not to create categories for your notes, but to let the notes categorize themselves based on how your brain thinks, and the types of ideas you're interested in. 
This is achieved by linking connected notes at the bottom and then viewing them later in the graph view.
As the notes and ideas accumulate, you will be able to see trends and connects from a top down view. What wasn’t obvious at the time each note was written, because obvious from the new perspective. Thus the idea is formed automatically and the work to support it is done

Works similarly to creating a personal Wikipedia of your own ideas or external ideas tailored for your interests.

**Part 1**
While reading, when you come across something interesting, thought provoking or surprising, make a note in either a journal or, on the kindle as it is easy to do so. The only rule is that the notes must be written in your own words. They cannot be quotes because this 1. Removes context from the quote which changes its meaning 2. Does not allow you to gain understanding or form connections with other personal thoughts. Then after you finish the book, read through those notes, turning them into better worded and formed notes, along with the references and connections to other ideas
The length of the notes we make is not not guided by any rule. Write as much or as little as you need to understand the text and form any connections with other ideas

It’s definitely the case that with some sort of reflection on the contents of a book, they slip from our mind almost as soon as the book is closed. That’s why readers, no matter if you plan to publish writing, should take notes while they read. Otherwise it is simply entertainment.
