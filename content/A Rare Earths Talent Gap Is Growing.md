# A Rare Earths Talent Gap Is Growing

The average age of the North American mining workforce is ~60, and facing retirement. 
At the same time, less students are entering the industry and demand for rare earths is increasing.
This is creating a scenario for a major talent gap, as well as a risk of losing key industry knowledge before it can be passed along.
