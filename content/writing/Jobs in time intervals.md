# Jobs in time intervals 
Created: 2024-03-19 08:00

Sam doesn’t think about jobs in terms of genre and whether they will be replaced with AI. 
E.g.  artist, accountant, etc

But rather what time frame of tasks can be replaced.
E.g 5 minute task, 1 hour tasks, 5 day tasks, 5 year tasks, etc.
