# Narrow Your Focus
Created: 16:57 16:57

One of the things I've realized since starting work is the necessity of keeping your focus and your goals narrow. We only have so much time in a day, in a week, in a year, and the more we spread out time around to different things, the less progress we make on any of them. The result, theoretically, could be catastrophic. The worst result would be a life without any specialized knowledge, and unique skill set, and no achievement.
This is similar to my experience in learning Italian. It was never a priority for me, so even though I was remarkably consistent. My consistency was made up of short amounts of time every day. 
The result was that I learned a decent amount of Italian words, could read, but even after 3 years, could barely get by. If I had focused my efforts on it, instead cutting out another focus of mine, I might be significantly better than I am now.
This is also why stopping Youtube is okay. I'm having a hard time accepting it, but its okay to narrow my focus. I think having 5 areas of true focus is a good number to have. This is what was recommended in Content Inc. and now experimenting with tracking my goals, I concur with.
**At the moment my goals are:**
1. Work
2. CPA
3. Blog
4. Fitness & Health
5. Relationships (Friends & Family)

Just looking at that, it would be basically impossible for me to fit anything else in, without causing terrible detriment to any of the others.
