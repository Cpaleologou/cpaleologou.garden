# Luhman's Productivity
Created: 11:22 11:22

Luhman was so productive partly because he never forced himself to do what he didn't feel like doing. If he didn't feel like working on a topic, he would just work on a different one instead. He was working with his emotions instead of trying to conquer them. 
THis meant that he was always working on numerous projects at the same time, which offered him many choices to focus on.
