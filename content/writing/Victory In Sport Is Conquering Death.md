# Victory In Sport Is Conquering Death

The enemy in sport, or a duel, is a representation of death. The physical and mental effort of the duel mimics the intense moment of pain, peril or panic that occurs when we face death. In this moment to win, requires one to escape from any consideration of the self. One must leave behind any speculation or stress for tomorrow. There can be no regard for the judgement of others. In this moment we are most alive, and in victory we become hero's in the conquering of death.
