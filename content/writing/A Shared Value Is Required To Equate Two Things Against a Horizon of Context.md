# A Shared Value Is Required To Equate Two Things Against a Horizon of Context

The culture of identity attempts to claim that all people are equal because they are different. It is in fact these differences that make them equal. 
However, this cannot be the case. Two different things can only be weighted equally if it is in reference to something else, a context, that we judge them against.

For example, a man and a woman, being different, are not "equal". However, when judged against a horizon of higher value, such as human dignity, we can say they are equal *despite* their differences.
