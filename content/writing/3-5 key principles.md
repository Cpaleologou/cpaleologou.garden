# 3-5 key principles
Created: 2024-04-11 07:06

I'm listening to a podcast on the Morgan-Hosell podcast.

The episode is Rules of the Money Game, and he begins by discussing a concept in another book where the author essentially talks about how when you first want to learn about a field, it seems like you have to memorize a whole bunch of different stuff, like tons and tons of stuff.

However, in reality, all you need to know is the three to five key principles that govern that field, and everything else is just some sort of combination or twist on those principles.
