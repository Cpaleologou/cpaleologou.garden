# Refined Information Flow - Charged Language

When selecting sources to include in the Refined Information Flow, the language used needs to be analyzed for bias.
Ideally, non-charged language should be used.

For example,
"but it is nonetheless a sign of Trump’s willingness to directly intervene in business after demanding 15% of Nvidia’s China revenues."

This is an example of what is not wanted. "Demands" as a verb insinuates a greed and power dynamic that is not validated from the rest of the piece. It carries a negative bias, unproven in the source material.
