# Zeitlkasten Method Boxes
Created: 2024-03-04 07:08

Three boxes: 
1. Permanent notes
3. Literature notes
4. Fleeting notes

**Permanent notes** are like blog posts which are finalized.
Literature notes are notes on interesting things that you are learning through content.
**Fleeting notes** are sort of random thoughts throughout the day that may be half-baked or just need to return to later.
