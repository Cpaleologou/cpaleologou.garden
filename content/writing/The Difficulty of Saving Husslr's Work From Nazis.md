# The Difficulty of Saving Husslr's Work From Nazis

Husslr, on his passing, left behind an enormous amount of work. As the second World War began to heat up, his family decided they needed to move this corpus out of the country where it would be safe from the book-burning practices of the Nazi party.
Husslr's compendium of notes and writings spanned over 60,000 pages, creating an enormous complexity problem in moving the work.
