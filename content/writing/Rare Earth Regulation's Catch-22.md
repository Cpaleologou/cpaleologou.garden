Environmental, Social and Governance movements successfully reduce demand for "conflict resources" in countries like the Congo. This in turn lowers the price producers attempt to sell the goods at in the marketplace. This actually advantages ethically unconcerned buyers, namely Chinese producers, who get to buy their materials at lower prices. Over time these producers, with better margins and thus greater capital to reinvest, out-complete ethically focused companies.
