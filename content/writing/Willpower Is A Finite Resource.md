# Willpower Is A Finite Resource
Created: 10:47 10:47

Despite what some people think, willpower, over the course of a day, is a finite resource that dwindles away as we use it. 

Questions:
Do some people have more will power than others?
Does willpower recharge faster for some people than others?
What activities recharge willpower the fastest?


---
## Quote

The implication is that some vital resource of the self becomes depleted by such acts of volition. To be sure, we assume that this resource is commonly replenished, although the factors that might hasten or delay the replenishment remain unknown, along with the precise nature of this resource.” (Baumeister et al., 1998, 1263f)
