# Habits Don't Enable A Full Life

There is an argument to be made that habit building is not an enabler of an authentic life. 
It's difficult to argue against the idea that good habits leads to domain specific success.
But to fill you day and your life with habits, leads to a kind of robotic mode of living without the variety and depth that comes from novelty and spontaneity.
