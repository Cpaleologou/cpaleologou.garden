## "Das Man" As A Cultural River

"das Man" is a concept describing the phenomenon of social momentum which, the current of a strong river, tends to pick us up in its rapids, carrying each us in whichever direction it seems to flow. 
Resisting "das Man", to instead follow our own virtues is to live authentically.
