# True Self-Awareness Requires Suffering

Self-awareness, as in a fundamental recognition of our own mortality can only be achieved when faced dead-on. 
In essence, a lethal strike from an enemy is metaphorically required to shock the body into self awareness. 
It is only in this moment when life can be recognized for its true value.
