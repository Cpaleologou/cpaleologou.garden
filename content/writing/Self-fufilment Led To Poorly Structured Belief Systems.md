# Self-fufilment Led To Poorly Structured Belief Systems

The increased bias of focus on the self in each individual results in belief systems that are less well-thought out, or time-tested. They are generally superfluous ideals that side-step concepts or issues that may be difficult to grasp because they lay beyond the self.

Two examples:
- The prioritization of career as a manifestation of self progression, over the family unit.
- The avoidance of involvement in discomforting global issues due to the stress they impose on the individual and their inability to affect it directly.
