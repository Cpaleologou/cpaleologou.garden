# The Authenticity Debate Ought To Be Over Definition, Rather Than Existence

Authenticity is a virtue. So the conversation about it should not be about whether it is or is not. Or whether it is good or bad. 
Instead, we should be focused on debating *what* it actually means to live an authentic life and how we should understand it.
