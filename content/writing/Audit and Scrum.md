# Audit and Scrum
Created: 2024-03-04 07:30

I think audit teams could really benefit from some of the elements of software dev management.
Both are sort of move fast, centre around the user/customer, and require working collaboratively in teams.
The Kanban board style of task tracking, especially could be useful as the team could track all tasks and see which tasks are being held up.
It would be a more transparent way of collaborating and potentially reduce distractions.
