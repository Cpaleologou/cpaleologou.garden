# Phenomenology

A philosophical study and movement largely associated with the early 20th century.
It is a practice that seeks to objectively investigate the nature of subjective, conscious experience and world-disclosure.
It attempts to describe, in words, the universal features of consciousness while avoiding assumptions about the external world, aiming to describe phenomena as they appear, and to explore the meaning and significance of lived experience.
