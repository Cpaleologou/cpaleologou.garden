# Life Is A Product Of Using Your Body

Many people, including myself have a tendency, or habitual preference, to retreat into their own minds. They spend their time with ideas, words, and creative expression. But life is experienced through the body - movement of the body. By ignoring the physical, we not only make ourselves weak and sick, but also steal the moments of life that make it worth living.
