# The Certainty of Vulcan's Existence

For 10-20 years, in the period of 1860-1870, the majority of the scientific community considered the planet Vulcan to be real. A, consequentially non-existent planet, hypothesized to orbit the sun within the orbit of Mercury. 
It was thought to be the only possible explanation to why Mercuries orbital path could not be perfectly predicted by Newtonian physics. 
The truth, was that the people of the time simply did not have all the information. They based their conclusion on what they knew, fitting an answer into the best framework that they had to explain the universe. 
Yet, the framework was incomplete. When Einstein published his new theory of physics, which solved the issue, Vulcan disappeared from the textbooks.
