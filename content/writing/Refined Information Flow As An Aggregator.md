# Refined Information Flow As An Aggregator

Social media acts as an aggregator of content. With everyone posting on the same platform, you can typically get what you are looking for from the app in just one place. Since we have the goal of leaving social media behind, the refined application flow needs to also be an aggregator. All the information I need should be in one place, one app.
