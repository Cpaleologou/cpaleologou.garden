# Technology May Not Be The Way to Fix Education

If you want to improve the education system, you might leverage tablets, AI tutors, and online learning platforms to make it more efficient and scalable.
But perhaps, with the skills and knowledge needed to excel in the world changing so rapidly, viewing education solely as information transfer is misguided.
Maybe, a better perspective is that education is human formation, and a larger focus should be placed on mentorship, dialogue and community.
