# Rare Earth Concentrations And Quality Have Been Diminishing

The concentration and quality of known REE deposits has been decreasing for decades. 

Concentration determines the bar required for a mine to be profitable, and quality determines the available use case and market for the processed REE.

This is leading to a situation where the low hanging fruit as been picked. *Ceteris paribus*, Time and energy costs will increase in the future.
