# Science Removed Our Explanations For Our Suffering

As our understanding of the world expanded, as science replaced religion as the dominant method of understanding the world, the mystique of the world was also lost.
With the loss of spirituality, we also lost our role in any kind of higher order or direction of the universe. And in losing that, we lost an explanation for why we suffer.
