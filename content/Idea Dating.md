# Idea Dating

A mindset to help set smaller goals, rather than making massive commitments such as:
"I'm going to start a Youtube Channel and post once a week"

You don't even know if you like it yet. Date the idea first. Instead say:
"I'm going to try Youtube. I'll make a channel and post once a week for 4 weeks. Then decide on making a longer commitment."
