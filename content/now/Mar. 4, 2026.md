Hello hello,

Looks like its been about a month since my last "Now" post. It's been pretty busy, but in a good way. 
## Career
The first thing I'm excited to share is that I've been interviewing for an internal promotion and new role, and I've gotten the offer! For the past year and a half I've been working as a Senior Financial Analyst I, supporting our merchandising business and some distribution related projects. It's been a fantastic learning experience, and I can honestly say that I love what I do. Specifically, I've had the opportunity to learn our Appliances business, our Wireless business (phones and activations), as well as the realities and complexities of our Canadian distribution network. 

This new role will be a promotion to SFA II, and will be focused on supporting our stores on a nation-wide level, as well as the financial analysis needs required on the labour that supports those stores. We have over 200 locations across Canada and truthfully I don't know anything about this part of our business, so it's going to be a massive learning exercise. 

The other exciting thing that comes along with the role is a somewhat irregular opportunity to work with and learn from some pretty senior leaders in the company. I'll be reporting directing to the Senior Director of finance which is quite a few levels above where I currently report into. It's an amazing chance to learn from really experienced people, with a lot of exposure to the top of the organization. That also obviously comes with more pressure and responsibility, especially as I try to learn the business on the fly, but I've learned over the last 5 years of my career that the best way to learn is to just jump right in.

## Fitness & Health

February, was catch up time. I followed it exactly, and I'm happy to say things are back on course for May, with last weekend marking my first half-marathon since last year. Unfortunately, I don't think I'm going to have enough training under my belt to hit my original time goal of 4 hours, but completing the race is good enough for me.
