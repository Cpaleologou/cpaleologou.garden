# Finding Yourself Requires Creation

We become *something* through our actions. If you act nice, you are nice. If you act mean, you are mean. 
When our actions *create* something new (to us, not necessarily the world), we discover new ways of existing and thus discover who we are. 
Is this way self-discovery requires *making*.
