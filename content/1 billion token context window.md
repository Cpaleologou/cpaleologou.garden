# 1 billion token context window
Created: 2024-03-06 07:00

The paper shows a way for transformers to be scaled to 1 billion+ context windows.
This is effectively being able to upload in a single interaction an entire library of books, or a massive code base.

Glacier journal lines: 2.1 million
Probably between 50-100million tokens
A 1billion token context window could easy sit on top of a corporations entire accounting system AND excel based working papers from the past AND have all of IFRS as a reference. 

The implication here is that businesses don’t need to create their own AI systems or do fine-tuning. It is more likely that there will be a huge few models from Microsoft, Amazon, etc. that you can just point at your entire business and ask it to work.
