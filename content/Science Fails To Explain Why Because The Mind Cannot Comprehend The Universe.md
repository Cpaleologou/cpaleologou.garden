# Science Fails To Explain Why Because The Mind Cannot Comprehend The Universe

Humans invented science to learn and understand what in the universe is true. 
Yet, as our understandings and explanations grow more encompassing, we reach a point where the mind cannot intuitively understand it (Think quantum physics). So we simplify it by creating models and images that our brains can comprehend. 
But, in that action, our understanding of the universe returns to "poetry".
