# Self-Fufilment Is Not Entirely Subjective

The modern schema of self-fulfilment general results in people holding deep connections with family, friends, our work, or other aspects of "ordinary life" in the highest regards. 
Notably, not faith or any other spiritualism.
This then results in the important finding that self-fulfilment is not entirely subjective to the individual, but requires some kind of recognition from others in the form of love, laughter, or success.
