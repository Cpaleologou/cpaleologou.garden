## Welcome to my digital field journal

I started writing online in 2021 to share book reviews and reflect on the things I thought would be interesting or actionable for friends, family, and myself.

Frankly, I've struggled with consistently posting over the years. I've always found it difficult to stay focused on a single topic or theme.

Recently, I discovered the growing trend of 'digital gardens', which feels much more aligned to my style of learning and sharing. These are opinionated, messy, linked, interconnected collections of notes and essays organized by _status_ rather than chronology or topic.

By status I am referring to the state of completeness. On each piece of writing here you'll see a tag indicating the completeness of the piece. Currently I am using the popular 3-tiered system of 'sapling', 'budding', and 'evergreen'.

### The System

**Jottings** are young, unfinished and, **importantly**, potentially wrong. These are closer to one-off ideas, observations, or notes, rather than finished works. Don't judge them too seriously.

**Entry** pieces are more developed. Here, a few ideas have been collected an synthesized. They aren't complete though. In fact, like jottings they are likely to change.

**Monograph** essays are going to be rare. These are largely finished works. A lot of time and thought will have gone into monograph content. You can interpret these as a fully formed thoughts, though I always maintain the right to change my mind!
