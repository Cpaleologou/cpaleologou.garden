Rare Earth Element mining is difficult, complex, and ripe with external risks. Most operations fail, leading to a high-risk high-reward casino-like environment.

The uncertainty scares off sophisticated investors who actually have the capital required to sustain successful long-term efforts of establishing a mining site.

For that reason, in the early stages, in order to establish sustainable mines, government funding may be needed.
