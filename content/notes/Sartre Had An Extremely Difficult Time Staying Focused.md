Sartre clearly suffered from extremely high openness without the balancing of conscientiousness needed to finish projects.
He would start projects constantly, discarding old ones mid-way through. This books often referenced a coming part 2 which was never delivered.
In the world of philosophy, he suffered from shiny-object syndrome, always wanting to explore something new.
