Social media acts as an aggregator of content. With everyone posting on the same platform, you can typically get what you are looking for from the app in just one place. This is a good thing and one of the few redeeming features of socail media.

A refined information flow should aim to mimic the best attributes of social media, while leaving the worst behind. A refined application flow therefore needs to also be an aggregator. All the information I need should be in one place, one app.
