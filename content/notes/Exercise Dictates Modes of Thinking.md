I seem to think in different ways when performing different kinds of exercise. 
When I'm walking for example, I am typically more relaxed. My mind kind of "unfolds". I am able to process a thought, think about it logically and move on.

When lifting though, I notice that my mind tends towards a more sporatic, almost ADHD way of creative thought. I have many different creative ideas that I need to constantly be writing down.
