The concentration and quality of known Rare Earth Element (REE) deposits has been decreasing for decades.

Concentration determines the bar required for a mine to be profitable, and quality determines the available use case and market for the processed REE.

This is leading to a situation where the low hanging fruit as been picked. _Ceteris paribus_, Time and energy costs will increase in the future.
