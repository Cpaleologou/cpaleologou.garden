In tennis, a pusher is a defensive style of player who strategically tries to acts as a human backboard rather than try to hit winning shots. They utilize deep strokes, dinks and lobs to consistently return the ball back to the opponent, putting the pressure on them to finish the match by taking riskier shots. Ultimately, the pusher wins by allowing the opponent to make unforced errors as over the course of the match have to make more difficult shots than the pusher.
