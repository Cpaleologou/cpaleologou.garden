In the myth of Sisyphus, Camus makes the argument for 'Don Juanism'. He argues that we should live life to the fullest and try to enjoy the present moment because we may not have tomorrow. 

My concern is that is this can quickly be misunderstood to lean into hedonism and pursuing pleasure in the moment, rather than seeking depth of experience in line with your values.
