A key pillar of whether something is authentic is that it is tested in the world through discourse with, and judgement of, others. 

An idea or creation cannot be judged by ChatGPT or other AI models that exhibit traits of sycophancy because they cannot be trusted to give a true opinion on the creation. 

Thus, they only they are not a form of dialogue with the world.
