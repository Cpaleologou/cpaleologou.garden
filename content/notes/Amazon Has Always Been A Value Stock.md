Amazon's P/E ratio vs. the market average, over the course of its entire history, has been high. On average, Amazon's P/E multiple has been 211x, while the market multiple has averaged 22x. 

![[WhereTheMoneyIs_AmazonValuationVsSnP500.webp]]

It has always looked expensive. On other key measures, the story is the same. GAAP profits and returns on invested capital have been negative. 

Yet, unlike many other tech executives, Bezos understood value and how to measure business performance. He knew the stock was a value play despite the usual metrics flashing red. How?

The question we must ask is how did Bezos measure the performance of his business despite being in the growth phase with low margins and poor ROIC?
