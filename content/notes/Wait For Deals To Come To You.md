Opportunistic investing means buying value that is being offered for low prices, not hunting for the next big thing.

When you try to identify the next big thing, you are in essence paying for a promise. A bargain, on the other hand, comes along when a seller is motivated and prices something below its value. 

A safe way to straddle being in the market and being opportunistic is to DCA into index funds, until an opportunity arises, when it does, either divert you DCA into the opportunity, sell to buy, or deploy cash reserves.
