"*Being and Nothingness*" and "*Being and Time*" from Sartre and Heidegger, respectively, are both unfinished. 

Both authors promise are part two or final conclusion, yet never delivered it, despite living for years after publication.Yet both works, despite their unfinished nature, made major impacts to the field of philosophy. 

Its a great example of why showing your work, even if unfinished, is important and can be valuable
