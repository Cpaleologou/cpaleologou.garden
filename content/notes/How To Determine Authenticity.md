Authenticity requires the following to be true:

1. Involves:
	- Creation, or discovery and learning
	- Originality, or the making of something in our own way
	- Pushing on the boundaries of generally acceptable behaviour.
		- Ask, *"would I be judged for this?"* Or, "*Am I embarrassed by this*". These are guides.
2. Requires:
	- Is not done **only** for fun. It sits in front of some other value or significant thing that gives it meaning.
	- The sharing or testing of the thing with others. Without discourse, any meaning is delusion.
