**î** and **ĵ**  (i-hat and j-hat) represent the "1" unit size on a graph. If you think about it then, a vector is actually a scalar of **î** and  **ĵ**. 

$\begin{bmatrix} 3 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix}$ therefore represents 3î + 2ĵ.

![[ihatjhatarebasisvectors.webp]]

That implies that the outcome of the vector can be significantly changed by changing the size of the basis vector, since it is arbitrary.
