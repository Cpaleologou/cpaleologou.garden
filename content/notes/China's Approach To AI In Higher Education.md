Many news outlets and university professors are raising the alarm that AI is destroying higher education by allowing students to cheat. 

Chinese universities, instead, are leaning into the new technology. 46 universities across the country are directly integrating AI use and education into existing programs, as well as creating new courses, facilities and degrees on the subject.

These are not just available to students in computer science or machine learning, but all students, despite their area of focus.
