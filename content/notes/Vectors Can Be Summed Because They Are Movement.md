The sum of two vectors can be visualized by taking the beginning of one vector and placing it at the end of another vector. 

$\begin{bmatrix} 1 \\ 2 \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} 3 \\ -1 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 4 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}$

## Connections

[[Vectors Represent Movement]]

**Link Explanation:**
The linked note describes vectors as instructions for movement. That is *why* they can be added to together. By moving along the instructions for the first movement, then moving along the instructions for the second movement, you end up at the same location you would have if you just traveled along their sum.
