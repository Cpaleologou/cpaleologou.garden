Cities seem to have some characteristics that are reminiscent of biological organisms. They are born, they grow, they produce waste, in some ways you could say they reproduce. However, they very rarely die. Many of the oldest cities around the world have survived despite being "moved", sacked, razed, emptied by plague, etc. Hiroshima and Nagasaki thrive, 70-or-so years after atomic bombs were dropped on them. 

Cities appear to be incredible examples of *durability*.
