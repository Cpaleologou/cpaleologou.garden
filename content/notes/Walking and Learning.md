Exercise itself moves information into our long term memory, that's why it's beneficial to take a walk as a study break.

---

## Quote

>"In fact, chances are it would help you more because exercise helps to transfer information into long-term memory (cf. Ratey 2008). Plus, exercise reduces stress, which is good, because stress floods our brains with hormones that suppress learning processes (Baram et al. 2008)."
