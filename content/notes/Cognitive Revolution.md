The Cognitive Revolution is the period of time in which the brain of Homo Sapiens and our ancestors grew rapidly and allowed us to develop a number of key abilities, including:

- Advanced tool use, 
- The ability to imagine abstract theories and concepts
- The ability to communicate extremely effectively with each other.
