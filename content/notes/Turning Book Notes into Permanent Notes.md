After finishing the book, review your exported notes to identify the most impactful ones.

Look for notes written in your own words that still catch your attention. If a note stands out during review, it means the idea resonated with you twice: once when you initially rephrased it, and again now. These double-resonance notes are candidates for permanent notes.

**To create a permanent note:**

- Rewrite it a third time with more detail
- Add connections to other ideas or notes
