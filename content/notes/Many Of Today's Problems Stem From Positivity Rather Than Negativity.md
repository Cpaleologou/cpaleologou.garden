Problems of our past could often be thought of as "negative" issues. Starvation or malnutrition caused by the **lack** of food. Plague or sickness caused by **external** forces. Today though, many of our largest issues come from "positivity". Obesity is a good example. It stems from **too much**. Too much food, too much fat, *too much of oneself*. 

Furthermore, in the past, our actions and decisions were governed by laws and cultural expectations that dictated what we **may not** do, or what we **should** do. Today this has shifted to a culture dictated by what we **can** do, or **could** do, if only we worked hard enough.

We tell children they can do whatever they dream of, if they work hard enough. Not wanting to be a failure we push ourselves harder than a dictator could ever achieve. You work 80 hour weeks because it's needed to "achieve your potential", rather than being forced to. It is a form of self slavery. It is rule by desire for the next carrot that can never be achieved, rather than the fear of a stick.
