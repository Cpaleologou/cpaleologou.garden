All ideological shifts are grassroots movements. They are not created at a university or government level and passed down. They must already exist in the minds of individuals. They may be defined at the scholarly or policy level, which further inspires their growth. But to have an impact, to stick, to get people on board requires that the idea already exist in the minds of the populace.
