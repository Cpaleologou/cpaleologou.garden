We often crave routines. We find comfort in practicable patterns of the day-to-day life. Yet, eventually, the routine drags on our souls. We question what we are trying to achieve, why it matters, and thus the purpose of our lives.Of course, no answer is apparent in this moment, and thus we first encounter the absurd.

In this way, the routine is the first step towards the inevitable intersect between our souls and the absurd.
