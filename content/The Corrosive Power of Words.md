# The Corrosive Power of Words

Words are invented to describe something that exists in reality. They are fundamentally a layer of abstraction from reality. When you describe something you are abstracting certain, but not all, qualities from that thing to communicate to others. As not all qualities can be abstracted through words, meaning is lost. In this way words are corrosive to true reality. As one describes more and more complex ideas using more and more words, they lose even more of the truth.
