# X (Twitter) Is More Interesting Than LinkedIn

X is vastly more interesting than LinkedIn because it is authentic. The culture of LinkedIn is full of people trying to be professional, or impressive. It's inherently not relatable because no one is being their true self. Everyone is hidding behind their suit, so to speak.
X, on the other hand, is full of people talking about their interests and passions with the same excitement, and imperfection that they embody in real life as a human. This makes it relatable and therefore interesting.
